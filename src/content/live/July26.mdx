This stretch covered two months but it felt like three different chapters. Site work, the series launch, and then Kerala.

## The Site 🛠️

June was a cleanup month. Sitemap, image compression, stripping out components that had no business being there anymore. The about page got reworked, the live page got restyled, page hero went full width. Small things on their own, but together they moved the site from something that worked to something that felt a bit more considered.

Then at the end of June, I saw a video from Emily Kowal showing a TOC interaction and it caught my attention. The kind of detail that changes how you navigate a long page without screaming for your attention. I built a version of it for the site and it works perfectly. Thats the best outcome for any interaction, it works and you stop thinking about it.

<TocPillDemo />

I also redesigned the series cards and the series index page, reworked the blockquote styling, and went through another round of typography unification. Typography is one of those things that never feels done. Every time I think I have it right, I open the site on a different screen and something looks off. But this round felt closer to settled than any before it.

## Prototyping in Code ✍️

The thinking from May about coded prototypes had been sitting with me for a while. In early July, I started writing about it.

The Prototyping in Code series went live on July 3. Its the first time I've tried to document an argument and a process at the same time, not just what I built but why the existing tools werent enough, and what changes when you prototype inside the actual environment the experience will live in.

I also rebuilt how series work on the site to support it. Related notes became dynamic, so each post in a series now surfaces the next part rather than a random selection. The series index got restructured. Small changes, but they make the reading experience feel sequential rather than scattered, which matters when you are trying to build an argument across multiple posts.

By July 12, the first posts were up and the structure was holding. And then I closed the laptop.

## Kerala 🌴

Went home from mid-July through August. A deliberate break, the kind where you dont bring the laptop mindset with you.

No commits, no prototypes, no typography fixes. Just home.
