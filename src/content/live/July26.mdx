This entry would be slightly different than what I used to log here. This entry is a composition of what happened in June and August. You may ask why? and my excuse is I was on a vacation.

## The Site 🛠️

June was more about being intellectually ambidextrous. On one hand, I was experimenting with prototyping in Unity within our production codebase (I wrote about it [here](/series/prototyping-in-code/) ), while on the other, I was deep in the weeds optimizing this website components, adding image compression and cleaning up the sitemap.

Then at the end of June, I saw a twitter post from a designer (Apologies, I forgot the name) showing a Table Of Content interaction and it was so beautiful that I was in awe. The kind of detail that changes how you navigate a long page without screaming for your attention. I built a version of it for this site and it works perfectly. Thats the best outcome for any interaction, it works and you stop thinking about it.

Along with TOC, there was another interaction I wanted to optimize in this site and that was about how the Series master card works in this site and For now, it seems solved as well. 


## Prototyping in Code ✍️

The thinking from May about coded prototypes had been sitting with me for a while. In late June, I started writing about it.

The Prototyping in Code series went live on July 3. Its the first time I've tried to document an argument and a process at the same time, not just what I built but why the existing tools weren’t enough for the scenarios I wanted to test, and what changes when you prototype inside the actual environment the experience will live in.

As I mentioned earlier, I rebuilt how series work on the site to support it. Related notes became dynamic, so each post in a series now surfaces the next part rather than a random selection. The series index got restructured. Small changes, but they make the reading experience feel sequential rather than scattered, which matters when you are trying to build an argument across multiple posts.
